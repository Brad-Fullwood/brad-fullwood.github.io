Privacy policy

Synchronised Photos is used only by Brad Fullwood's household and is not offered to the public.

With the Google Photos access you grant, the app uploads photos and videos from the household's own Immich server into your Google Photos library and creates albums for them. It lists only the albums it created itself. It does not read, download or share any other content from your Google account.

The sign in token is stored on the household server and is not sent anywhere else. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Remove the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
